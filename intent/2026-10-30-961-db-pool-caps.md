# Intent: Cap PFactory's DB connection pools so 4 replicas can't exhaust Postgres

## Problem

Each PFactory pod can open up to 38 Postgres connections: the main SQLAlchemy
engine at library defaults (5 + 10 overflow = 15), JobStateStore (5 + 5 = 10),
PlanSessionStore (5 + 5 = 10) and the WS relay (asyncpg pool max 2 plus one
LISTEN connection = 3). The shared Postgres allows 97 usable connections.
factory-gitops#319 raises PFactory's ceiling to 4 replicas: 152 by PFactory alone.
A burst could starve AIFactory, TFactory and KEDA's scaler. A saturated pool
also waits 30 s (SQLAlchemy default) before erroring.

## Proposed outcome

Each pod's ceiling is small, explicit and settable from env; 4 pods fit in 97
with room for the other services. A saturated pool fails within seconds with
SQLAlchemy's "QueuePool limit ... reached" error. 1–2 pods behave as today
(measured use is 3 connections per pod).

## Affected users and systems

PFactory web-server pods on the shared k3d Postgres; AIFactory, TFactory and
KEDA indirectly. Read-only check of AIFactory and TFactory engine settings.

## Constraints

- No gitops or cluster change; no deploy. SQLite local dev must keep working.
- Must land before factory-gitops#319 leaves draft.
